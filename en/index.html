Direction 01

Chemicals for the pulp and paper industry

Eight product groups covering the wet end, the drying section and downstream converting.

Yankee cylinder chemicalsPetrofer

The Petrofer system: dosing equipment and a set of chemicals controlling tissue paper parameters. COTAC H — base coating that protects the Yankee surface and provides adhesion; RELEASE AGENT — mineral or vegetable oil based release for better sheet removal; COTAC C — phosphate-containing modifier protecting the surface and preventing corrosion; COTAC 460P/480P — edge treatment preventing carbon build-up.

Dyes and fixatives

Direct anionic and basic cationic dyes for bleached cellulose fibre. Fixing agents improve the retention of dye, fibre and fillers and increase rub-off resistance.

145C WSAwet-strength resin

Highly concentrated cationic polyamide-epichlorohydrin resin. Improves strength, breaking length, burst resistance and abrasion resistance.

DeCotac / Repulpaserepulping

DeCotac — a strong oxidising agent for repulping wet-strength broke. Repulpase — an enzyme-based product that repulps at neutral pH.

Feroclean / StickStopcleaning

Feroclean — alkaline and acidic formulations for cleaning pipelines, wires and felts. StickStop — passivation of the paper machine wire that prevents deposition of stickies and other contaminants.

Enzymes and deinking

Enzymes for fibre modification improve stock dewatering, retention and physical-mechanical properties. Deinking chemistry is selected for the recovered paper grade and the installation layout.

Barrier coatingsSchill + Seilacher

Fluorine-free polymer coatings for board and speciality papers: Ukaphob (hydrophobic and oleophobic), Ukaphob 5200 (anti-slip), Ukaprint (flexo-printable barrier), Ukadur (mechanical protection), Ukacross/Ukasol (crosslinking accelerators), Despumol (defoamers), Ukadur X (fire-resistant).

Direction 02

Activated carbons and adsorbents

Carbons in all forms and molecular sieves for nitrogen and oxygen generators, hydrogen purification, gas drying, drinking water treatment and wastewater treatment.

Activated carbons

Crushed — drinking water, wastewater, condensate treatment at refineries, cigarette filters. Granular — solvent vapour recovery, air and gas purification, wastewater. Powdered — wastewater, pharmaceutical industry. Impregnated — biogas and landfill gas purification, drinking water. Catalytic grades available on request.

Shirasagi MSC CT-350nitrogen

Universal carbon molecular sieve for nitrogen generators, used to produce high-purity nitrogen. Packaging: drums of 137 kg (200 l).

CarboTech CMS H2hydrogen

Molecular sieve for hydrogen purification by PSA technology — downstream of steam reformers, catalytic reformers and ethylene plants.

Köstrolith (CWK)zeolite

Zeolite molecular sieves for oxygen generators, air and gas dryers and hydrogen purification units.

Ceycarb CEWC 1240in stock

Coconut shell based granular activated carbon by Core Carbons. Fine pore system for removing organic micro-pollutants and excess chlorine from drinking water; excellent hardness. Available from our warehouse in the Moscow region.

Direction 03

Filter materials

Activated-carbon nonwovens and extruded carbon honeycombs for industrial and domestic air purification.

Activated carbon filter media160–475 g/m²

Two layers of nonwoven material with the adsorbent placed between them; carbon content from 160 to 475 g/m². Excellent adsorption properties and good pleatability. Can be produced with special carbons for hydrogen sulphide removal. Used for industrial and domestic air purification and automotive cabin filters.

Helsa Sorbexx CScarbon honeycomb

Produced by extruding a homogeneous mixture of powdered activated carbon and a ceramic binder. High adsorption efficiency, mechanical strength and no dusting. Applications: exhaust and ventilation air purification, cleanroom technology, HVAC, vehicles, household appliances, respiratory protection, water treatment and tobacco smoke filtration.
